# Pre-submit checklist (App Store)

Tick a box only after checking it for real.

- [ ] The name includes the target keyword and is 30 characters or fewer.
- [ ] The subtitle is 30 characters or fewer and does not repeat the name.
- [ ] The keyword field is 100 bytes or fewer, with no words already used in the name or subtitle.
- [ ] The one-sentence difference is visible in the name, subtitle or first screenshot.
- [ ] The first two screenshots show the one job the app does.
- [ ] The core job works without making anyone create an account first.
- [ ] The payment or subscription is set up and tested, so revenue can be measured from day one.
- [ ] A privacy policy exists at a public URL and matches what the app collects.
- [ ] The app asks for the rating prompt after a good moment, not on first open.
- [ ] Every screen in PLAN.md works on a real device or simulator.
- [ ] You know where to read downloads, conversion and proceeds: App Store Connect Analytics, Sales and Trends, Benchmarks.
